# Fandom Specific AUs

> [!info]- Muse Backstory Rewritten To Fit Within Different Fandom's Universe
> 
> While *technically* [Crossovers](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/Crossover), these AUs discard the muse's Canon Typical backstory in favor of rewriting to fit within a different fandom's universe — the muse is then considered *native* to the new universe and has no ties with or knowledge of their origin fandom whatsoever. Muses from their origin fandom *are* welcome to interact with them in these AUs, but unless plotted otherwise, my muse will likely have no idea who your muse is, nor will they recognize any references to other characters or events outside of the Fandom Specific AU's fandom setting.
> 
> In most cases, the rewritten backstories will reflect many of the key points to the muse's personal history from their original canon, but only insofar as the new setting allows. This also applies to Combat Class and Abilities/Powers, reflective of what is available in universe with the closest approximation to what they are able to achieve in their origin canon.
>

# Displaced Universe AUs

> [!info]- Muse Displaced To Different Fandom's Universe
> 
> These AUs are essentially [Crossovers](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/Crossover) in the truest sense, in that the muse is *usually* their Canon Typical self (unless another AU is specifically requested otherwise), who have been displaced from their universe of origin and inexplicably find themselves in an unknown-to-them universe, whether completely alien or even familiar yet very clearly *not* their own (in cases of their being in the AU for their own origin fandom).
>
